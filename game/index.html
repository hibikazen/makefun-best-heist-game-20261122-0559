How to play

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys, or click the floor to walk there.
  • Goal: grab the gold loot, then reach the green EXIT with it.
  • Cones: amber cones are guards, red cones are cameras. If one holds you, the alarm fills. Break line of sight and it drains.
  • Spotted: at 100% you get a short warning — break away in time or you are caught.
  • Shadow lanes (dark floor) halve how far cones see. Vents (grates) hide you completely but slow you down.
  • Tools: 1 smoke bomb, 2 noise-maker, 3 hack. Each costs points and has limited uses.
  • Keys: P pause, R restart room, M mute.
